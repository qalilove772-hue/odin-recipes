Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with a flat dough base topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and other toppings. It is baked until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted

ingredient

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni
  • Onion
  • Bell pepper
  • Mushrooms
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 450°F (230°C).
  2. Roll the pizza dough into a flat circle.
  3. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  5. Add pepperoni, onion, bell pepper, and mushrooms.
  6. Sprinkle Italian seasoning and a little olive oil on top.
  7. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.
  8. Let the pizza cool slightly, then slice and serve.
