Description

Oven-baked chicken is a simple and delicious meal made by seasoning chicken with herbs and spices and baking it until it is tender and golden on the outside.

ingredient

  • Chicken pieces
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic
  • Lemon juice
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Paprika
  • Onion powder
  • Garlic powder
  • Dried oregano
  • Dried thyme

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Place the chicken pieces in a baking dish.
  3. Mix the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, salt, pepper, paprika, and herbs in a small bowl.
  4. Rub the seasoning mixture over the chicken pieces.
  5. Bake the chicken until it is fully cooked and golden on the outside.
  6. Remove the chicken from the oven and let it rest for a few minutes.
  7. Serve the chicken while it is warm.
