Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, cheese, and tomato sauce. It is baked until the cheese becomes melted and golden.

ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the ground beef with onion and garlic until browned.
  3. Add the tomato sauce and Italian seasoning, then simmer.
  4. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  5. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan cheese.
  6. Repeat the layers until the ingredients are used.
  7. Bake for about 40 minutes until the cheese is melted and golden.
  8. Let the lasagna cool for a few minutes before serving.
